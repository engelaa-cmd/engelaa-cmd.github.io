Gunggeumhanyang (궁금하냥)

Gunggeumhanyang is a small desktop tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel 궁금하냥 (Gunggeumhanyang), to upload short vertical videos that explain scientific principles to that channel. The channel discloses its use of AI.

The tool is used only for the owner's own channel. No other users sign in, and it does not collect anyone else's data.
